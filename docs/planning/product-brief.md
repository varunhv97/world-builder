# Product Brief

## Goal

Help solo creators turn imagined geography into a usable, enjoyable digital workspace that feels tangible and creator-owned.

## V1 scope

- Responsive browser application with private, account-owned worlds.
- Multiple worlds, each beginning as one finite interactive 3D terrain area.
- A generated base with a preset, landmass, mountain intensity, water level, roughness, and reproducible seed.
- Direct terrain sculpting; surface/biome painting; editable points, paths, and areas; rivers; mountain ridges; and contextual properties.
- Search/discovery, terrain-aware advisory realism feedback, undo/redo, deletion confirmation, local recovery, portable `.loka` snapshots, and cloud-backed persistence.

V1 deliberately excludes lore/relationship management, characters or events, collaboration, public sharing/discovery, native apps, generic import/export, and AI-assisted expansion.

## Constraints

The map is an immersive 3D workspace, not a globe-scale simulator. Adjacent-area expansion is a future capability. Geographic realism warnings guide a creator but do not prevent intentional exceptions.
